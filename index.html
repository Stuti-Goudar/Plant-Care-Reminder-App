    Plant Care Tips

    ☀️ Sunlight Requirements

    Place indoor plants near bright, indirect light. Rotate pots weekly for even growth.

    💧 Watering Tips

    Check the top 2 inches of soil before watering. Ensure pots have proper drainage holes.

    🧪 Fertilizer Information

    Use balanced liquid fertilizer every 3–4 weeks during active growing seasons.
